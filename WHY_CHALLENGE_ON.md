# Why Challenge On?

Challenge On is not a direct replacement for Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts. It is the challenge layer that gives short-form participation clear rules, ranking, verification, and rewards.

## Core Positioning

- `Challenge On` is the user-facing challenge experience.
- `U&On` is the broader brand and holder identity.
- `UNON` is the token and reward unit.
- Challenge tags should include `#UNON #challengeon` instead of legacy `#UNON #challengeon`.

## Why Users Open Challenge On

1. Prize-backed official challenges give users a concrete reason to enter through the app before posting elsewhere.
2. Regional and global rankings turn scattered short-form videos into structured competitions.
3. WorldID verification and holder badges make participation more trustworthy than a generic hashtag feed.
4. Long-term challenge records, badges, and rankings create persistent status beyond a single video upload.

Challenge On should remain focused on the challenge operating layer: rules, registration, scoring, verification, rewards, and discovery.
